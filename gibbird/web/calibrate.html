Camera calibration

← Birds

  1. Reference points: pick one flat surface where birds land (e.g. the face of the railing, or the floor). Click 4 or more spots on it that you can measure, then type each spot's position in cm (e.g. railing's left end = 0,0; right end = 300,0; with Y going up or across). 5+ points give an error estimate.
  2. Zones: click Draw zone, click around the area where birds should be detected, then Finish zone.
  3. Save. The white grid shows 50 cm squares on your surface. If it doesn't line up, fix the points.
